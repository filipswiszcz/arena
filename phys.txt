GAME PHYSICS:

actor struct:
    - global pos (vec2)
    - rigidybody (struct)
    - collider (struct)
    - bool grounded

rigidbody struct:
    - vel, acc (vec2)
    - ?mass, drag (float)
    - grav (bool)
    - ?accu force (vec2)

collider struct:
    - local pos (vec2)
    - min/max (vec2)
    - dir (int32_t [4]) // [N, E, S, W]
